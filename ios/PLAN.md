# Rebuild Emperial Bot — Trading & Market Analysis App

Based on the file you uploaded, here's the full Emperial Bot app rebuilt as a native iOS experience:

---

**Features**

- **Dashboard** — Overview of your portfolio, P&L metrics, win rate, and bot health status at a glance
- **Broker Integrations** — Cards for TradingView, NinjaTrader, R Pro Trader, Binance, Discord, and Telegram with webhook endpoint info for each
- **Bot Management** — View and toggle trading bots and arbitrage bots on/off, see their P&L and performance stats
- **Signals & Alerts** — Configurable alert engine with conditions like price thresholds, EMA crossovers, golden/death cross, ATR, RMP, and pressure flip signals
- **Help / Tutorial** — Built-in guide covering all app features and how to use them

---

**Design**

- Dark, sleek theme with a professional trading-app feel
- Deep black/dark gray background with accent colors (green for profit, red for loss)
- Cards with subtle borders and rounded corners for each section
- SF Symbols for all icons (chart icons, bell for alerts, gear for settings, etc.)
- Clean typography hierarchy with bold headers and subdued labels

---

**Screens**

1. **Dashboard** — Main screen showing portfolio summary, active bots count, recent signals, and quick stats
2. **Brokers** — Grid/list of connected broker platforms with webhook details and connection status
3. **Bots** — List of trading/arbitrage bots with toggle switches, P&L display, win rate, and health indicators
4. **Signals** — Alert configuration screen with condition types, thresholds, and active alerts list
5. **Help** — Expandable accordion-style guide explaining each feature
6. **Tab Bar** — Bottom navigation to switch between Dashboard, Brokers, Bots, Signals, and Help
